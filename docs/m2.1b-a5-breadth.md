## Coverage limits and unchanged gates

This exercise tests direct and aliased inheritance sensitivity for four defined
host-owned objects. Together with A4 it still cannot prove absence of every
possible capability, resistance to privileged external mutation, post-resume
behavior or complete Gate A. Trust in the Windows kernel, trusted host and
no external privileged handle-table mutation remains explicit. Two stable
snapshots and retained lifetimes constrain races within this defined model.

A2 remaining synthetic semaphore, independently opened same-file object and
wrong-pipe controls (12/13/14), mutation (15), resumed-child (16), wall termination
(17), and inspection stall/deadline-failure behavior require a separate decision.
No test-only exception may enter production code.

write denial, explicit positive-control writes, sealed identities, protected
objects, Job restrictions, independent wall supervision, bounded contracts,
producer/verifier separation and fail-closed evidence remain unchanged. Ambient
read and periodic CPU-enforcement exceptions remain confined to the accepted
fixed M2.1A operation. There is no additional security exception.

grants, production journal, Stop/recovery, independently verified publication,

remain gated. No plan, import, digest, verification or green CI grants authority.
