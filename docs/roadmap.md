# Roadmap

The [public source launch](public-launch.md), independent disclosure review,
repository safeguards and four-job hosted matrix are complete as of 2026-10-28.

1. Review the v0.1 presentation and release-note drafts with Product. Seek separate
   owner approval for a formal release and external announcement.
2. Define a portable specification/proposal schema with exact source provenance,
   duplicate reconciliation and a clear separation from execution authority.
3. Prove bounded real transports: finite in-flight exposure, input/output accounting,
   no hidden retries, crash reconciliation and provider cancellation semantics.
4. Integrate independently verified identity and intent with isolated signing,
   revocation and rollback-resistant recovery. A familiar login is not sufficient.
5. Review live admission, stop, budget and continuation together before a narrowly
   bounded owner-approved pilot. Notifications and remote dashboards follow their
   own authenticated installation and privacy review.

These are requirements and research directions, not committed delivery dates.
Passing offline tests does not remove the blockers for live use.
